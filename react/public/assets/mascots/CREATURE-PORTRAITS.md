# Distinct attendee portraits

Generated with built-in imagegen in text-to-image mode, one transparent asset per character. Original PNGs are preserved in `png/`; q88 WebP copies in `webp/` power the UI. These replace the similar student/mentor robot portraits for demo attendees. Hacky keeps the original knitted robot. Student and Mentor labels identify roles independently of color or accessories.

Asset: `png/hacky-fox.png` and `webp/hacky-fox.webp`.

Energetic slight tilt.

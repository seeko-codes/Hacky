# Knight Hacks IX event guide

Checked 2026-10-11 against the official site, Devpost, and the Notion hacker's guide and schedule. All times are Eastern (EDT). Where sources disagreed, the hacker's guide schedule wins (it is the newest).

## Basics
- 36-hour, in-person hackathon run by Knight Hacks (a UCF student organization), Friday October 9 to Sunday October 11, 2026, at UCF main campus in Orlando, Florida. Hub: Engineering II (ENG2) Atrium.
- Hacking runs Fri 11:00 PM to Sun 11:00 AM. The event ends with the closing ceremony, Sun 6:00–7:30 PM.
- Free for accepted hackers; meals, snacks and drinks are included. No experience needed, beginners welcome.
- Eligibility: Devpost's rules say college students aged 18+; the website FAQ also welcomes people who graduated in the past year.
- You can come and go; staying the whole time isn't required.

## Check-in, badges and classes
- Check-in and swag pickup: Fri 4:00–6:45 PM in ENG2 102. Late check-in: not published (ask at the organizer desk in the ENG2 Atrium).
- Bring a photo ID and your Blade QR code from the Hacker Dashboard (https://2026.knighthacks.org/dashboard). The QR code is scanned at events and meals.
- Each hacker gets a class lanyard. Bloom side: Whisperseed, Dryad, Petalborn. Blight side: Chomper, Spora, Collosus. The lanyard is your meal pass, must be worn at all times, and is not replaced if lost.

## Venue and rooms
- ENG2 Atrium: main hub with the organizer, mentor and MLH help desks.
- Hacking: BA1 107 and BA1 239; BA1 121 (louder); BA1 218 (quiet lo-fi room).
- Workshops: ENG2 102, ENG2 203, BA1 119. Indoor socials: BA1 146.
- Meals: ENG2 105 and ENG2 205. Sleep: ENG1 224, 327 and 427.
- Girls Who Code safe space: BA2 210. Sensory room: BA2 207. AMD Lounge: BA1 225.

- Floor maps are on the Hacker Dashboard. Unlisted rooms, and buildings after hours, are off-limits and can mean dismissal. The library and other public areas are fine while open.

## Parking and getting there
- With a UCF permit: nothing to do. Without one: the driver requests an event parking code once, then re-enters it in the UCF parking portal every day, even if the car never moves. Park nose-in. Portal: https://secure.parking.ucf.edu/ParkingPortal/Events/EventDefault.aspx
- Which lot to use: not published. There are no buses and no travel reimbursement.
- Packages: the UCF Amazon Pickup location is about a 5-minute walk (Sunday 11 AM–5 PM).

Anyone with a severe allergy should ask the vendor or an organizer.

## Submission and judging
- Devpost: https://knight-hacks-ix.devpost.com/ . Hard deadline Sunday 10:00 AM, no late submissions; edits allowed until 11:00 AM. Final code push by 11:00 AM; no repo changes after that.
- Required: every teammate on the Devpost project (anyone missing gets no prize), a demo video, the prizes you're opting into, and a public GitHub repo (private or missing repo means disqualification).
- Teams of up to 4; solo is allowed. One project per team, started after hacking begins (no past projects).
- Judging: Sunday 12:00–4:00 PM. Claim your project on the Hacker Dashboard with the emailed link; it then shows your time, room and track. Visit the General room plus each sponsor room you opted into (sponsor rooms are announced Sunday morning). At least one member must attend.
- Each slot: 2 minutes setup, 6 minutes judging, 2 minutes teardown, hard stop. If a slot doesn't work, tell an organizer beforehand; missing one without notice disqualifies you from that challenge and all General tracks.
- Criteria: originality, technical understanding, functionality, design, and wow factor.

## Rules and code of conduct
- Knight Hacks and MLH codes of conduct apply: be respectful and inclusive. Harassment, discrimination, threats, weapons, hate symbols, recording people without consent, disrupting talks, cheating or sabotage are banned.
- Minor issues get a warning; serious or repeat issues mean removal and a ban from Knight Hacks and MLH events, plus possible UCF discipline.
- Code of conduct: https://knight-hacks.notion.site/code-of-conduct and https://mlh.io/code-of-conduct

On paying
attention

We often think attention means looking harder. But sometimes it means making room.

Consider a walk without a destination. When there is nowhere you need to arrive, small things begin to appear: light moving across a wall, a pause in someone’s voice, the shape of a familiar street.

Nothing has been added to the world. Something has been removed from the way we look at it.

Perhaps attention is less about effort and more about the willingness to stay with what is already here.
